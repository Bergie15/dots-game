Settings

Bubble size

Bigger bubbles means fewer on the board. Each mode remembers its own size. Changing size starts a new board.

Popping

Tap twice shows the points before popping.

How to play

  • Tap 2 or more touching bubbles of the same color to pop them.
  • More bubbles, more score: each bubble is worth 10 more than the last (5, 15, 25…).
  • Complete a stage to earn coins: 1, or 2 with fewer than 10 bubbles left, 5 with fewer than 5, and 10 for clearing the board.
  • Spend coins on powers: 🔨 crush a 3×3 area (20), ⭐ turn a 3×3 area one color (50), 🌈 change one bubble's color (100).
